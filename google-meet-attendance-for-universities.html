Attendance Tracker

Google Meet attendance tracker for universities & colleges

Managing lecture attendance for 100+ university students across multiple course sections and semesters is a massive administrative headache. Here is how higher education professors and academic departments automate class attendance in Google Meet.

Trusted by Academic Faculty Globally: Used across state universities and colleges in the Philippines, India, Nigeria, Argentina, Malaysia, and the United States without requiring IT department intervention.

Challenges of Taking Attendance in Higher Education

  • Massive Lecture Sizes: When 150 students join an online lecture, taking manual roll call is physically impossible.
  • Proxy Sign-Ins & Drop-offs: Students frequently sign in, turn off their camera, and leave after 5 minutes. Professors need to see exact minutes stayed.
  • Academic Accreditation & Financial Aid Audits: Universities must maintain verified student attendance records to comply with accreditation boards and federal student aid requirements.

How University Faculty Use Attendance Tracker

1. Zero-Friction in Large Lectures

The add-on operates directly within the Google Meet side panel. It effortlessly processes high-concurrency lectures with 50 to 250+ students, recording exact join timestamps, leave timestamps, and total minutes present.

2. Teaching Assistant (TA) Delegation

Unlike Google's built-in tool (which only sends reports to the original meeting creator), Attendance Tracker works for any attendee. TAs and co-instructors can run the add-on from their own accounts and export reports to their personal Google Drive while the lead professor conducts the lecture.

3. Multi-Session Semester Course Roll-Ups

University courses meet 2–3 times per week for 16 weeks. Attendance Tracker groups all lectures by course ID and generates a cumulative Class Summary tab showing:

  • Each student's cumulative attendance percentage across the entire semester
  • Total lecture hours logged per student
  • Early warning flags for students at risk of failing attendance minimums

4. Department & Campus Deployment

University IT administrators can deploy Attendance Tracker domain-wide across all faculty accounts in 30 seconds via the Google Workspace Admin Console, or professors can install it individually with zero administrative approvals required.
